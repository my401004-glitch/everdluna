### 3. AI 이미지 프롬프트 구체화 (Production Workbook)

다음은 위 스토리보드에 맞춰 Midjourney 또는 Stable Diffusion 등에 입력할 수 있도록 구체화된 프롬프트입니다. 시각 스타일(Dark Blue/Accent Yellow)을 반드시 반영합니다.

#### A. 인트로 / 문제 제기 씬 프롬프트
**Prompt 1 (Hook Scene):**
> Cinematic close-up shot of a student's face, deeply frustrated and anxious, illuminated by harsh shadows. Background is deep dark blue (#0A2463). Accent yellow lines subtly overlay the frame, representing confusion. High contrast, cinematic lighting, ultra-detailed, volumetric light. --ar 16:9 --style raw

**Prompt 2 (Pain Visualization):**
> Abstract visualization of tangled musical notes and unstable waveforms in a dark blue environment. The lines are chaotic and broken. Sharp accents of bright yellow highlight the points of instability. Minimalist, data-driven aesthetic, high detail. --ar 16:9

#### B. 시스템 제시 / 해결책 씬 프롬프트
**Prompt 3 (System Flow):**
> A clean, structured flow diagram illustrating a transformation process. Start with chaotic input (Dark Blue) and transition through clear, structured steps (Accent Yellow lines) into a final optimized result. The design must look like a mathematical or engineering blueprint. Minimalist UI style. --ar 16:9

**Prompt 4 (Gain Visualization):**
> A bright, optimistic scene. A single beam of intense golden yellow light shines through a dark blue structure, illuminating a perfectly aligned musical scale or waveform. Symbolizing clarity and achieved mastery. Soft volumetric lighting, high fidelity. --ar 16:9
